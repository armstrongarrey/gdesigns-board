Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how G-DESIGNS LTD ("we," "us," or "our"), the operator of Arreyon Consult, collects, uses, and protects information when you use our platform at consult.gdesignsme.com and related services (the "Service").

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, phone number (optional), country, and a password (stored as a secure hash, never in plain text). If you sign up using Google, we receive your name, email, and profile picture from Google.

Business Information You Provide

When you use features like the Website Analyzer, Market Research, Entrepreneur Mode, or the Boardroom, you may provide information about your business — such as your industry, location, challenges, financial estimates, and website URL. We use this information to generate the advice, reports, and analysis you request.

Payment Information

When you subscribe to a paid plan, you provide payment details through our payment process. We do not store full payment card numbers on our own servers.

Google Analytics Data (if you connect it)

If you choose to connect your Google Analytics account, we request read-only access to your Analytics data (such as sessions, users, conversions, and traffic sources) via Google's official API. We use this data solely to display it in your dashboard and, where you use features that reference it, to ground reports and recommendations in your real business performance. We do not sell, share, or use this data for advertising purposes. You can disconnect this access at any time from your dashboard, which stops any further data access.

Automatically Collected Information

We use a login session cookie to keep you signed in. We do not use third-party advertising trackers or sell browsing data to advertisers.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, operate, and improve the Service
  • To generate AI-powered business advice, reports, and analysis based on the information you provide
  • To process payments and manage your subscription
  • To communicate with you — account verification, password resets, service updates, and (if you're on Pro or Business) team invitations
  • To display your connected Google Analytics data back to you within the Service
  • To monitor and improve the reliability and security of the Service

3. How Your Information Is Shared

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in these circumstances:

  • AI providers: to generate advice and reports, the business information you provide is sent to Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI (ChatGPT), and/or Google (Gemini), depending on which AI model is used for a given request. These providers process this data to generate a response but do not use it to train their models on our behalf, per our agreements with them.
  • Service providers: we use third-party providers for email delivery, hosting, and database services, who process data only as necessary to provide those services to us.
  • Team members: if you're on a Pro or Business plan and invite team members, the business data, reports, and Analytics data associated with your account become visible to those team members, according to the permissions you grant them.
  • Legal requirements: if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of G-DESIGNS LTD, our users, or others.

4. Data Retention

We retain your account information and business data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal information within a reasonable period, except where we're required to retain it for legal or accounting purposes.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Disconnect any third-party integration (such as Google Analytics) at any time
  • Export your data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

6. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, and access controls. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States and other jurisdictions where our service providers operate. We take steps to ensure your information is treated securely wherever it is processed.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you by email or through a notice on the Service. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
